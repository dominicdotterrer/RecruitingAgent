## Domain Expertise

### Differential Privacy and Information Theory
Developed mathematically verifiable methods for assessing statistical leakage risk in aggregated market data and ML models trained on sensitive client transactions. Built a decoder-based synthetic market data generation service with automated calibration of the leakage-utility tradeoff. Architected federated learning regimes for privately training models across sensitive data partitions. Two published artifacts in this area (paper + patent).

### Physical Process Monitoring — Semiconductor Manufacturing
At Performance-Star, developed ML applications monitoring deposition and plasma etch processes in semiconductor fabrication equipment. Provided novelty detection, hidden state detection, and signal modeling from raw process traces. Required deep integration with plasma physics and control theory. Led a team of 6 data scientists and engineers that required rapid upskilling in advanced physical and mathematical concepts.

### Quantitative Finance and Stochastic Processes
At Goldman Sachs: built optimization tools for trading desks and prime brokerage, including hedging strategies with dynamic risk limits and productionizing optimal order execution algorithms. Earlier R&D work (MITACS fellowship) on exotic options pricing models for complex employee stock compensation. Deep familiarity with stochastic differential equations, market microstructure, latent state estimation, and information-theoretic market models.

### Agentic AI Systems and LLM Architecture
Designed and deployed LLM-based systems at scale: LLM guardrails (NeMo, LlamaGuard) for identifying and blocking MNPI leakage in RAG systems, agentic skills architectures, MCP tooling, retrieval systems. Developed quality evaluation frameworks for agentic code generation. Transitioned production model services from single-pass generation to agentic architectures.

### Graph Theory and Network Topology
First-principles graph theory background (spectral graph theory, zig-zag products, coboundary expansion, higher-dimensional simplicial complex theory). Published in this area. Directly applicable to network topology design, routing optimization, geospatial inference, and ISR network problems.
